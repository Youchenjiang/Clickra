# Privacy Policy for Clickra

**Last Updated:** April 27, 2026

## Overview
This Privacy Policy describes how Clickra ("the App") handles your data.

## Data Collection and Usage
**Clickra does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, telemetry, or analytics data.** 

The App is designed as a completely local utility. All file processing operations, including but not limited to:
- PDF merging
- PPTX to PDF conversion
- Image to PDF conversion
- Image stitching and merging

are performed **100% locally on your device**. 

## Third-Party Services
Clickra does not integrate with any third-party cloud services, tracking SDKs, or analytics platforms. The App does not require an internet connection to function.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the GitHub repository: https://github.com/YouchenJiang/Clickra
