# Privacy Policy for Clickra

**Last Updated:** April 27, 2026

## Overview
This Privacy Policy describes how Clickra ("the App") handles your data.

## Data Collection and Usage
**Clickra does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, telemetry, or analytics data.** 

The App is designed as a local utility. Most file processing operations (PDF merging, Office-to-PDF conversion, image conversion/stitching) are performed **100% locally on your device** and do not require an internet connection.

### Exception for PDF Translation
If you choose to use the **PDF Translation** feature, the App transmits the text extracted from the PDF securely over HTTPS to the Google Translate API (`translate.googleapis.com`) to perform the translation. This text is sent solely for the purpose of translation and is not collected, stored, or used by Clickra.

## Third-Party Services
Except for the optional PDF Translation feature communicating securely with Google Translate, Clickra does not integrate with any third-party cloud services, tracking SDKs, or analytics platforms.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the GitHub repository: https://github.com/YouchenJiang/Clickra
