## Product Name
Clickra

## Description
Clickra is a lightweight context menu utility for Windows File Explorer.

Process files directly from the right-click menu:
• Office to PDF: Word, Excel, and PowerPoint
• PDF Toolkit: Compress, decrypt, split, and merge
• Image Tools: Format conversion, stitching, and PDF packaging
• Markdown: Convert .md to PDF or Word
• Paper Translation: PDF translation with formula preservation

All processing runs 100% locally on your PC. Your files never leave your machine.
Free, open source, and zero background memory.

Source Code: https://github.com/Youchenjiang/Clickra
